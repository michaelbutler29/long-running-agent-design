# Long-Running Agent Design

Companion artifacts for the *Long-Running Agents* article series on LinkedIn.

## The Series

A four-part series on designing long-running agents, exploring human nature as a design heuristic. The series covers four structural dimensions: role and purpose, security as policy, skills and growth, and designed well-being.

## What's in this Repo

Each article in the series includes a practical artifact: a diagnostic, template, pattern, or reference you can take into your next build. This repo hosts those artifacts.
